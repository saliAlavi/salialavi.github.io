Hearing aids amplify every voice alike

More than 1.5 billion people have hearing loss. In a crowded room a device needs to know which talker you are following. The answer is in your brain, your eyes and the turn of your head.

Merit: make every modality earn its share.

A multimodal attention decoder and a test of what each input contributes: shuffle one stream, measure the accuracy drop, split it with Shapley values. Adding gaze and head motion raises accuracy but nearly halves the brain’s share of it.

Sep 2026 · arXiv

NEUROTOKEN

One EEG network with three heads: which talker, which side, and the attended envelope. A conditional flow-matching head scores each candidate talker by likelihood ratio instead of a single noisy correlation.

Sep 2026 · Submitted to IEEE TASLP

MAESTRO, an open multimodal corpus

The first auditory-attention dataset to record EEG, eye gaze, pupillometry, egocentric video and head IMU at once, with four competing talkers and background noise across several SNRs. I designed and built the EEG + eye-tracking recording setup: IRB protocol, stimulus delivery, hardware sync and participant sessions.
